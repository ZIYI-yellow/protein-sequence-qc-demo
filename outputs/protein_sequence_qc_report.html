Scientific boundary

This small reviewed-reference benchmark is not a toxin classifier, does not analyse a published genome and does not claim discovery of a novel venom peptide or therapeutic lead. The records mix precursor proteins with fragment or mature-peptide entries, so their lengths and signal-peptide status are not directly comparable.

This reference set mixes precursor proteins with fragment or mature-peptide records. Their lengths and signal-peptide status are therefore not directly comparable. A project-stage analysis should first standardise the sequence level, then use supervisor-approved species, genomic data type, toxin reference set, signal-peptide and transmembrane tools, homology and domain analysis, and an experimental validation plan.
